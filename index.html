BACKGROUND & DISCIPLINE

THINKING IN SYSTEMS.
BUILDING WITH RIGOR.

I am a systems engineer and creative technologist driven by the desire to make software feel physically tangible, blisteringly fast, and aesthetically uncompromised.

My work spans the spectrum from bare-metal C++ systems, high-speed radio frequency analysis, and digital signal processing (FFT, symbol synchronization in GNU Radio), to tactile modern web engineering using WebGL, shaders, and custom UI audio synthesis.

Inspired by the high-craft design engineering of interfaces like haoqi.design and industrial computing hardware, I believe the best engineering happens when technical excellence and sensory design work together without friction.
