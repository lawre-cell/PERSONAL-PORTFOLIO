// 01. BIOGRAPHY & NARRATIVE

BUILDER & STORYTELLER AT THE INTERSECTION OF CODE, DATA & VERSE

I am Lawrence Holali Eklah, a builder and storyteller navigating the intersection of software engineering, data science, and spoken word poetry. Currently studying Computer Science and Software Engineering at the University of Ghana in Accra, I approach problem-solving with both analytical rigor and creative intuition. Whether I am leading technology and systems for The Ascend Network, building solutions through data science, or stepping onto a stage as a spoken word artist, I view code, data, and verse as distinct languages for making sense of complex human experiences.

My technical work centers on architecting scalable, full-stack systems and designing data-driven solutions from the ground up. As an independent software engineer and technical leader, I thrive on turning ambitious ideas into functional software—working across modern stacks like Next.js, TypeScript, Express, and PostgreSQL, while building custom object-oriented data structures in Java. From engineering shared availability engines and release pipelines for vehicle management platforms to integrating payment webhooks and conducting field research on campus transit bottlenecks, I am driven by the challenge of building resilient systems that solve real-world operational problems. At the same time, my artistic drive pushes me to strip away technical abstraction and connect directly with people through rhythm, narrative, and performance poetry.

Looking ahead, my vision is to continue bridging analytical systems with human-centered expression. I am actively exploring the frontiers of artificial intelligence, interactive state-driven web architectures, and data-informed community building through hackathons, venture coaching, and grassroots tech leadership. Whether I am shipping production code, analyzing complex datasets, or crafting a new spoken word piece, my goal remains the same: to engineer tools and tell stories that empower communities and leave a lasting impact.
